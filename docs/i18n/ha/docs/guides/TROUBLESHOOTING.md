## Cikakken Jagorar Warware Matsaloli

---

### Takaita Ƙimar Buƙatu a Masu Bayarwa na Kyauta (429 / 400 / 401)

**Alama**: Lokacin amfani da `model: "auto"` tare da masu bayarwa na kyauta/marasa buƙatar tabbatar da izini (opencode, auggie, da sauransu), lokaci-lokaci kana samun `HTTP 429`, `400`, ko `401` maimakon amsoshi. Buƙatun suna yin nasara idan aka sake gwada saƙon nan take bayan ɗan lokaci, amma sarrafa aiki ta atomatik (ayyukan cron, agents, scripts) yana katsewa a gazawar farko.

**Asalin matsalar**: Hanyoyin gazawa guda uku masu zaman kansu suna haɗuwa:

1. **Iyakar ƙimar mai bayarwa (`429`)**: Matakan kyauta na iya tilasta ƙa'idar amfani ta kowace tazara. Aika tarin kiraye-kiraye lokaci guda yana ƙare ta, don haka za a ƙi buƙata ta gaba har sai tazarar ta sake farawa.
2. **Model mai matsala a passthrough (`400`/`401`)**: Rukunin `auto/*` na iya ƙunsar passthrough models daga `opencode` waɗanda aka yi wa rajista a catalog amma ba su da ingantattun credentials masu aiki (misali `oc/north-mini-code-free` → `401`). Auto-router yana gwada ɗaya, ya gaza, sannan kuskuren ya bazu kafin fallback ya fara aiki.
3. **Ƙaruwar haɗin-aiki-lokaci-guda (`429` a ƙarƙashin nauyi)**: Lokacin da zaman agent/cron da yawa suka kira `auto` lokaci guda, jimillar ƙimar buƙatun tana wuce abin da masu bayarwa na kyauta za su iya jurewa, don haka ana yi wa sahihan kiraye-kiraye alama a matsayin cin zarafi.

**Gyaran da aka tabbatar (al'umma ta bayar da rahoto, 2026-08-10)**: daidaita environment variables guda uku domin rotation, concurrency, da fallback su shawo kan sauye-sauyen matakin kyauta maimakon tsarin ya mutu a kansu:

```bash
export OMNIROUTE_ROTATE_ON_400=true           # tsallaka zuwa wani model/mai bayarwa idan an samu 400/401 (yana tsallake passthrough models masu matsala)
export OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT=4   # bayyananniyar iyakar karɓar manyan ayyuka (ba a saita ta ta tsohuwa: babu iyakar adadin buƙatu, duba bayanin da ke ƙasa)
export OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_QUEUE_MS=5000 # jira mai iyaka na tsawon lokaci don samun damar babban aiki maimakon retryable 503 nan take
```

Saita waɗannan a muhallin aikin OmniRoute (daemon ɗin, misali ta LaunchAgent plist ko `systemctl edit`), sannan ka sake kunna OmniRoute. Rotation flag shi ne mataki mafi tasiri: yana sauya gazawa kai tsaye zuwa sake gwadawa a ɓoye ta hannun ingantaccen mai bayarwa da ke cikin rukunin.

**Lura**: `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT` yana iyakance adadin manyan buƙatu — masu dogon context — da za su gudana lokaci guda; iyakar ƙofar karɓa ce, ba mai takaita ƙimar mai bayarwa ba. **Sabuntawar #503-fanout:** ba a ƙara saita wannan var ta tsohuwa (yanzu yana aiki ne kawai idan an saita shi kai tsaye, kamar yadda ke sama) — maimakon haka, ana sarrafa karɓar manyan ayyuka ta byte budget da aka samo ta atomatik (`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_INFLIGHT_BYTES`) wanda ke daidaita kansa bisa ainihin iyakar memory ta host, don haka sabon deployment ya kamata ya ga ƙarancin ƙin buƙatu na `503 chat_admission_busy` sosai ba tare da saita wannan var kwata-kwata ba; saita shi kai tsaye a nan har yanzu yana aiki daidai kamar yadda aka rubuta. Bayyanannun sauye-sauyen byte-budget suna takaita zuwa 8 MiB–2 GiB. `413 body_exceeds_budget` ba matsala ta wucin gadi ba ce: ƙara wannan byte budget, rage `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_HARD_MAX_BODY_BYTES`, ko ƙara iyakar memory ta process. Watsarwar `inflight_bytes_budget` cunkoso ne na wucin gadi kuma har yanzu ana iya sake gwadawa. Ana sarrafa takaita ƙimar kowane mai bayarwa (`open-sse/services/rateLimitManager.ts`) daban ta `RATE_LIMIT_MAX_WAIT_MS`, `RATE_LIMIT_MAX_QUEUE_DEPTH`, da `RATE_LIMIT_AUTO_ENABLE` — duba `.env.example`.

**Yadda za a tabbatar gyaran ya yi aiki**: gudanar da agent/cron ɗinka sau biyu cikin gajeren lokaci sannan ka tabbatar dukansu sun yi nasara. Kafin gyaran, gudu na biyu yawanci yana jefa `429`/`401`. Bayan gyaran, ana sake gwada gazawa (idan akwai) a ɓoye kuma kiran yana kammala. Haka kuma za ka iya yin `curl /monitoring/health` sannan ka lura da filin `rateLimitedUntil` a haɗin masu bayarwa da kuma `circuitBreakers.providerBreakers[].state` na masu bayarwar da abin ya shafa — state ɗin yana ɗaya daga cikin `CLOSED`, `DEGRADED`, `OPEN`, ko `HALF_OPEN` (duba `src/shared/utils/circuitBreaker.ts`), kuma mai bayarwar da ke ci gaba da gazawa zai sauya `CLOSED → DEGRADED → OPEN` kafin tazarar sake farawa ta ba da damar probe ya wuce (`HALF_OPEN`).

**Idan har yanzu kana ganin 429**: asusun da yake aiki na wannan mai samarwa ya ƙare da gaske daga _quota_ ɗinsa (ba iyakar yawan buƙatu kawai ba). Ƙara asusu na biyu na wannan mai samarwa a OmniRoute dashboard → Providers → Accounts, ko haɗa wani mai samarwa na kyauta (misali `routeway`, `auggie`). Juyawa tsakanin asusu yana taimakawa ne kawai ga matsalolin iyakar yawan buƙatu na ɗan lokaci/400/401; ƙarewar quota gaba ɗaya na buƙatar bayanan shaidar shiga na biyu ko wani mai samarwa daban.

**Idan kana ganin 403 a kan vision models (`auto/vision`, `bazaarlink/*`)**: asusun da aka haɗa ba shi da tsarin biyan kuɗi da ya ƙunshi vision, ko kuma API key ɗin ba shi da isassun izini. Tabbatar a dashboard na mai samarwa cewa key scope ɗin ya ƙunshi vision/multimodal, ko ka haɗa asusun matakin biyan kuɗi sannan ka riƙe shi a matsayin vision target.

## Gargadin npm install (ERESOLVE / peer / deprecated)

Lokacin da ka gudanar da `npm install -g omniroute`, za ka iya ganin jerin gargadi masu yawa kamar `npm warn ERESOLVE`, sanarwar peer-dependency, da saƙonnin `deprecated`. **Ana tsammanin waɗannan kuma ba su da illa.** Shigarwar ta yi nasara idan ka ga `added <N> packages` a cikin sakamakon.

Gargadin suna fitowa ne daga tsofaffin kewayon peer-dependency a cikin fakitin wasu ɓangarori waɗanda OmniRoute ba ya sarrafawa:

1. **`marked-terminal` yana buƙatar `marked >=1 <16`, amma an sami `marked@18`** — yana aiki yadda ya kamata a aikace; kawai dai kewayon peer na upstream ya tsufa.
2. **`deprecated prebuild-install@7.1.3`** — mataimaki ne na ɗauko native-binary wanda ya zo ta hanyar wata dependency. Ba a
   amfani da shi wajen shigar da transport binding na `wreq-js` da aka ƙayyade sigarsa, kuma ba ya nuna cewa saitin jigilar
   mai samar da web-cookie ya gaza.

**Ba a buƙatar wani mataki** — ba za a iya kashe gargadin gaba ɗaya ba sai an yi fork na fakitin upstream.

## Ganewar Ƙarya ta Riga-kafi

<a name="antivirus-false-positives"></a>

### Avast/AVG na keɓe `README.md` da `MD:HttpRequest-inf[Susp]`

**Wannan ganewar ƙarya ce. Babu abin da ya kamu da cuta, kuma ba a buƙatar ɗaukar wani mataki.**

Avast da AVG suna amfani da hanyar hasashe wadda ke yi wa fayilolin rubutu na yau da kullum/Markdown
masu ɗauke da hanyoyin haɗi da yawa masu kama da buƙatun HTTP alama. `README.md` na OmniRoute yana
zuwa cikin kunshin npm (an jera shi a `package.json` → `files`), don haka yana sauka a
`node_modules/omniroute/README.md` yayin shigarwa ta global — kuma yana ɗauke da misalai kusan 15 na
`http://localhost:20128/...` (endpoints na MCP HTTP/SSE, URL na A2A `.well-known`, da
misalan `curl`). Yawan waɗannan hanyoyin haɗin ya isa ya tayar da wannan hanyar hasashe.

Idan wannan ya fara faruwa kwanan nan ne kawai: nau'in fayil ɗin bai canza ba. README ya faɗaɗa
teburin endpoints ɗinsa (an ƙara MCP HTTP + SSE + A2A) da ƙarin misalan `curl`, wanda ya sa
ya wuce iyakar da aka saita.

Fayil ɗin takardu ne kawai marar aiki, ba shi da wani abun da za a iya aiwatarwa. Za ka iya mayar da shi
daga keɓewa ba tare da haɗari ba.

**Abin da za a yi:**

1. **Dakatar da sanarwar** — cire kundin shigarwar daga binciken riga-kafinka
   (Avast: Settings → Exceptions), ta ƙara hanyar global `node_modules` ɗinka da/ko
   kundin bayanan OmniRoute (`~/.omniroute/`).
2. **Kai rahoton ganewar ƙarya** — <https://www.avast.com/false-positive-file-form.php>,
   tare da haɗa `README.md` da aka keɓe. Wannan shi ne gyaran da zai taimaki kowa, domin
   hanyar hasashen mai samarwar ce ke yin matsanancin martani ga fayil ɗin rubutu.

**Dalilin da ya sa ba ma “gyara” wannan daga ɓangarenmu:** duk misalan `http://localhost` ne, kuma
localhost ba zai iya zama `https` ba tare da wahalar takardar shaida mai sanya wa kai hannu ba. Gurɓata takardun domin
kauce wa hanyar hasashen mai samarwa guda ɗaya zai cutar da kowane mai karatu don biyan buƙatar kuskuren na’urar bincike.

### Kaspersky na yi wa manhajar Desktop alama a matsayin `PDM:Trojan.Win32.Generic`

**Wannan ganewar ƙarya ce daga hanyar hasashen ɗabi'a. Babu abin da ya kamu da cuta.** Prefix ɗin Kaspersky na
`PDM:` yana nufin hukuncin ya fito daga Proactive Defense Module (System Watcher) ɗinsa,
wanda ke tantance abin da mai shigarwar _yake yi_ maimakon kwatanta shi da sanannun manhajojin cutarwa. Idan
ya kunna, Kaspersky yana “mayar da baya” duk shigarwar — yana share fayilolin da ya riga ya
rubuta — don haka manhajar tana ƙarewa da lalacewa ko ɓacewa.

Fayilolin da yake yi wa alama sassa ne na asali daga sanannun dependencies masu buɗaɗɗen tushe da aka haɗa da
manhajar desktop, misali:

- `resources/app/.build/next/node_modules/playwright-<hash>/lib/…/agentParser.js` da
  `workerProcessEntry.js` — [Playwright](https://playwright.dev), library na sarrafa browser ta atomatik
  da ake amfani da shi don shiga provider daga cikin manhajar da kuma chat mai amfani da browser.
- `resources/app/.build/next/node_modules/@wreq-js/binding-win32-<arch>-msvc-<hash>/wreq-js.win32-<arch>-msvc.node`
  — native binding na `wreq-js` da aka kulle sigarsa, wanda ake amfani da shi don HTTP mai kwaikwayon sawun browser a kan providers masu amfani da web-cookie
  (`<arch>` shi ne `x64` ko `arm64`).

**Dalilin da ya sa yake kunnawa:** **har yanzu ba a sanya wa** mai shigarwar Windows hannu da code-signing ba, don haka mai shigarwar NSIS
marar sa hannu ba shi da wani suna na amincewa, kuma hanyoyin hasashen ɗabi'a suna aiki da tsananin ƙarfi. Idan aka haɗa
wannan da native DLL da aka kunsa da ɗaruruwan fayilolin `.js` da ake rubutawa a ƙarƙashin
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\OmniRoute` (ciki har da kundin kunshin masu ƙarin hash daga
Next.js standalone build), wannan ya isa ya tayar da hanyar hasashen. An shirya yin code signing;
har sai an aiwatar da shi, sabbin releases na iya maimaita wannan.

**Abin da za a yi:**

1. **Da farko ka tabbatar da download ɗinka** (wannan yana kawar da yiwuwar an sauya fayil ɗin ba bisa izini ba). Kowane release yana wallafa
   `latest.yml`, wanda filin `sha512` ɗinsa (base64) ke rufe mai shigarwar
   `OmniRoute.Setup.<version>.exe`. A cikin PowerShell, daga folder ɗin da ke ɗauke da mai shigarwar:
   ```powershell
   $b = [System.Security.Cryptography.SHA512]::Create().ComputeHash(
     [System.IO.File]::ReadAllBytes("$PWD\OmniRoute.Setup.<version>.exe"))
   [Convert]::ToBase64String($b)
   ```
   Dole ne output ɗin ya yi daidai da `latest.yml` → `sha512`. Idan bai yi daidai ba, share fayil ɗin sannan
   ka sake download daga [shafin releases na GitHub](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/releases) kawai.
2. **Mayar + cire daga bincike** — mayar da abubuwan da aka share yayin mayar da baya daga keɓewa sannan ka ƙara exclusion
   ga `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\OmniRoute` (Kaspersky → Settings → Threats and Exclusions),
   sannan ka sake shigarwa.
3. **Kai rahoton ganewar ƙarya** — <https://opentip.kaspersky.com/>. Rahotannin FP
   da masu amfani suka aika suna taimakawa sosai wajen hanzarta allowlisting.

## Daidaituwar Node.js

<a name="nodejs-compatibility"></a>

### Shafin shiga yana rushewa ko yana nuna kuskuren "Module self-registration"

**Dalili:** Kana amfani da sigar Node.js da ba ta cikin mafi ƙarancin amintaccen yanayin aiki da OmniRoute ya amince da shi. Yanayin da aka fi samu shi ne amfani da tsohuwar sigar gyaran Node 22 ko 24 wadda take ƙasa da mafi ƙarancin matakin tsaron da OmniRoute ke buƙata.

**Alamomi:**

- Shafin shiga yana nuna farin allo ko kuskuren sabar
- Console yana nuna `Error: Module did not self-register` ko makamantan kurakuran native binding
- Shafin shiga yana nuna **tutar gargaɗi mai launin lemu** tare da sigar Node ɗinka idan yanayin aikin yana wajen ƙa'idar tsaro da ake tallafawa

**Gyara:**

1. Shigar da sigar Node.js LTS da ake tallafawa (shawara: Node.js 24.x):
   ```bash
   nvm install 24
   nvm use 24
   ```
2. Tabbatar da sigarka: `node --version` ya kamata ya nuna `v24.0.0` ko sabuwa a jerin 24.x LTS
3. Sake shigar da OmniRoute: `npm install -g omniroute`
4. Sake kunnawa: `omniroute`

> **Amintattun sigogin da ake tallafawa:** `>=22.22.2 <23` ko `>=24.0.0 <27`. Ana tallafawa Node.js 24.x LTS (Krypton) da Node.js 26 gaba ɗaya.

### npm v11+: Ba a shigar da `better-sqlite3` ba (Cannot find module)

<a name="npm-v11-better-sqlite3-not-installed-cannot-find-module"></a>

**Dalili:** npm v11 (wanda yake zuwa tare da Node.js 24+) yana hana rubutun shigarwa na optional
dependencies ta tsohuwa. Saboda an jera `better-sqlite3` a cikin `optionalDependencies`
kuma yana buƙatar native compilation (`node-gyp rebuild`), npm yana tsallake shi ba tare da sanarwa ba.

**Alamomi:**

- Sabar tana rushewa yayin farawa tare da `Cannot find module 'better-sqlite3'`
- `ls node_modules/better-sqlite3` yana nuna "No such file or directory"
- `npm ls better-sqlite3` yana nuna `(empty)`

**Gyara:**

1. Amince da rubutun shigarwa sannan ka sake shigarwa:
   ```bash
   npm approve-scripts better-sqlite3
   npm install
   ```
2. Ko ka shigar da prebuilt ɗin da hannu:
   ```bash
   npm pack better-sqlite3@13.0.1
   tar -xzf better-sqlite3-*.tgz -C node_modules
   mv node_modules/package node_modules/better-sqlite3
   rm better-sqlite3-*.tgz
   ```
3. Tabbatar yana aiki: `node -e "require('better-sqlite3')(':memory:').close(); console.log('OK')"`

### macOS: `dlopen` / "slice is not valid mach-o file"

<a name="macos-native-module-rebuild"></a>

**Dalili:** Bayan yin `npm install -g omniroute` na gama-gari, wataƙila an haɗa native binary na `better-sqlite3` da ke cikin package ɗin don wata architecture ko Node.js ABI daban da wanda ke aiki a na'urarka. Wannan ya zama ruwan dare a macOS (duka Apple Silicon da Intel) idan pre-built binary bai dace da muhallinka ba.

**Alamomi:**

- Sabar tana kasa aiki nan take yayin farawa tare da kuskuren `dlopen`
- Kuskuren yana ƙunshe da `slice is not valid mach-o file`
- Cikakken misali:

```
dlopen(/Users/<user>/.nvm/versions/node/v24.14.1/lib/node_modules/omniroute/app/node_modules/better-sqlite3/build/Release/better_sqlite3.node, 0x0001): tried: '...' (slice is not valid mach-o file)
```

**Gyara — sake ginawa don muhallinka na gida (ba sai an koma tsohuwar sigar Node.js ba):**

```bash
cd $(npm root -g)/omniroute/app
npm rebuild better-sqlite3
omniroute
```

> **Lura:** Wannan yana sake haɗa native binding ɗin da sigar Node.js da CPU architecture na na'urarka, yana warware rashin daidaituwar binary. Kewayon yanayin aikin da ake tallafawa a hukumance shi ne **`>=22.22.2 <23` ko `>=24.0.0 <27`** (`SUPPORTED_NODE_RANGE` a cikin `src/shared/utils/nodeRuntimeSupport.ts`, wanda aka daidaita da filin `engines` na `package.json`). Ana tallafawa Node.js 24.x LTS (Krypton) da Node.js 26 gaba ɗaya tare da `better-sqlite3` v12.x.

## Matsalolin Proxy

<a name="proxy-issues"></a>

### Tabbatar da mai samarwa yana nuna "fetch failed"

**Dalili:** A baya, endpoint ɗin tabbatar da maɓallin API (`POST /api/providers/validate`) yana ƙetare saitunan proxy, wanda ke haifar da gazawa a mahallai da ke buƙatar tura zirga-zirga ta proxy.

**Gyara (v3.5.5+):** Yanzu an gyara wannan. Hanyoyin tabbatar da mai samarwa suna bi ta `runWithProxyContext`, tare da mutunta saitunan proxy na mai samarwa da na duniya ta atomatik.

### Binciken lafiyar token yana gazawa da "fetch failed"

**Dalili:** Sabunta token na OAuth da ke gudana a bayan fage ba ya gano saitunan proxy na kowace haɗi.

**Gyara (v3.5.5+):** Yanzu mai tsara binciken lafiyar token yana gano saitunan proxy na kowace haɗi kafin yunƙurin sabuntawa. Sabunta zuwa v3.5.5+.

### Proxy na SOCKS5 yana mayar da "invalid onRequestStart method"

**Dalili:** A Node.js 22, dispatcher na undici@8 bai dace da ginannen aiwatarwar `fetch()` ta Node ba.

**Gyara (v3.5.5+):** Yanzu OmniRoute yana amfani da aikin `fetch()` na undici da kansa idan dispatcher na proxy yana aiki, don tabbatar da daidaitaccen aiki. Sabunta zuwa v3.5.5+.

### Proxy na MITM a ƙarƙashin WSL: ba a kama ƙa'idodin tebur da ke kan Windows host

**Dalili:** Ana shigar da proxy na MITM da takardar shaidarsa ta CA a cikin mahallin da OmniRoute ke gudana. A ƙarƙashin WSL, wannan muhalli shi ne Linux guest, yayin da ƙa'idodin AI na tebur (Kiro, Trae, Copilot, Zed, …) suke gudana a kan Windows host. Ƙa'idodin host ba sa amincewa da ma'ajiyar takardun shaida ta guest kuma ba sa bi ta proxy na tsarin guest, don haka kamawar zirga-zirgar ƙa'idodin tebur ba ya aiki a can.

**Shawara:** Gudanar da OmniRoute kai tsaye a kan OS ɗin da ƙa'idodin tebur ɗin da kake son kama zirga-zirgarsu suke gudana (Windows don ƙa'idodin Windows; haka ma macOS/Linux). Barin OmniRoute a cikin WSL yayin da ake niyyar ƙa'idodin host yana buƙatar amincewa da takardar shaidar CA da aka samar da hannu a kan Windows host, sannan a saita saitunan hanyar sadarwa/proxy na kowace ƙa'idar host zuwa endpoint ɗin proxy na WSL — wannan tsari ne marar tallafi kuma mai saurin lalacewa.

## Matsalolin Docker

### Docker IPv6 / Sake Saitin Haɗi

<a name="docker-ipv6"></a>

**Alamomi:** `curl http://localhost:20128/v1/models` yana mayar da `curl: (56) Recv failure: Connection reset by peer`. Dashboard da endpoints marasa tantancewa suna aiki, amma endpoints masu tantancewa suna gaza — yana kama da matsalar auth amma ba haka ba ne.

**Dalili:** `docker run -p 20128:20128` yana buɗewa a kan duka `0.0.0.0` (IPv4) da `::` (IPv6), amma process ɗin da ke cikin container yana sauraro a kan IPv4 kaɗai. A hosts inda `localhost` ya fara resolve zuwa `::1`, haɗin yana sauka a published port na IPv6 ba tare da listener a bayansa ba → sake saitin haɗi.

**Gyara:**

1. **Bincike mai sauri:** Gudanar da `curl -4 http://localhost:20128/v1/models`. Idan yana aiki da `-4` amma yana gaza ba tare da shi ba, kana da rashin daidaiton bind na IPv6.
2. **Gyara na dindindin:** Yi bind zuwa IPv4 kai tsaye ta amfani da `-p 127.0.0.1:20128:20128` a cikin umarnin `docker run` ɗinka:
   ```bash
   docker run -d --name omniroute --restart unless-stopped --stop-timeout 40 \
     -p 127.0.0.1:20128:20128 -v omniroute-data:/app/data diegosouzapw/omniroute:latest
   ```
   Wannan yana tilasta bind na IPv4 kuma yana hana fallasa proxy a kan dukkan interfaces na host.

## Gano Kurakurai

### Kunna Fayilolin Log

Saita `APP_LOG_TO_FILE=true` a cikin fayil ɗin `.env` ɗinka. Ana rubuta logs na manhajar a ƙarƙashin `logs/`.
Ana adana artifacts na request a ƙarƙashin `${DATA_DIR}/call_logs/` lokacin da aka kunna pipeline na call log
a cikin saituna.
Lokacin da aka kunna pipeline capture, saita `CALL_LOG_PIPELINE_CAPTURE_STREAM_CHUNKS=false` don cire
payloads na stream chunks, ko daidaita `CALL_LOG_PIPELINE_MAX_SIZE_KB` don canza iyakar artifact a KB.

### Duba Lafiyar Provider

```bash
# Dashboard na lafiya
http://localhost:20128/dashboard/health

# Binciken lafiyar API
curl http://localhost:20128/api/monitoring/health
```

### Ma'ajiyar Runtime

- Babban state: `${DATA_DIR}/storage.sqlite` (providers, combos, aliases, keys, settings)
- Amfani: Tables na SQLite a cikin `storage.sqlite` (`usage_history`, `call_logs`, `proxy_logs`) + `${DATA_DIR}/call_logs/` na zaɓi
- Logs na manhajar: `<repo>/logs/...` (lokacin da `APP_LOG_TO_FILE=true`)
- Artifacts na call log: `${DATA_DIR}/call_logs/YYYY-MM-DD/...` lokacin da aka kunna pipeline na call log

Aikin **Clean history** na shafin Request Logs yana share `call_logs`, tsohon
`request_detail_logs`, da directory na artifact na cikin gida `${DATA_DIR}/call_logs/`.

| **Mai Sa Ido Kai Tsaye** | Kalli zirga-zirgar buƙatu a ainihin lokaci don gano matsalolin fassara da ke faruwa lokaci-lokaci      |

### Matsalolin tsari da aka fi samu

- **Thinking tags ba sa bayyana** — Duba ko mai samarwar da ake nufi yana goyan bayan thinking da saitin kasafin thinking
- **Tool calls suna ɓacewa** — Wasu fassarorin tsari na iya cire filayen da ba a goyan baya; tabbatar da hakan a yanayin Filin Gwaji
- **System prompt ya ɓace** — Claude da Gemini suna sarrafa system prompts ta hanyoyi daban-daban; duba sakamakon fassarar
- **SDK yana dawo da raw string maimakon object** — An warware a v1.x; mai tsabtace amsa yana cire filayen da ba na daidaitaccen tsari ba (`x_groq`, `usage_breakdown`, da sauransu) waɗanda ke haifar da gazawar tabbatarwar Pydantic ta OpenAI SDK. Idan har yanzu kana ganin wannan a v3.x+, da fatan za ka shigar da rahoton matsala.
- **GLM/ERNIE yana ƙin rawar `system`** — An warware a v1.x; mai daidaita rawa yana haɗa saƙonnin system cikin saƙonnin user ta atomatik ga samfuran da ba su dace ba. Idan har yanzu kana ganin wannan a v3.x+, da fatan za ka shigar da rahoton matsala.
- **Ba a gane rawar `developer` ba** — An warware a v1.x; ana sauya ta atomatik zuwa `system` ga masu samarwa da ba OpenAI ba. Idan har yanzu kana ganin wannan a v3.x+, da fatan za ka shigar da rahoton matsala.
- **`json_schema` ba ya aiki da Gemini** — An warware a v1.x; yanzu ana sauya `response_format` zuwa `responseMimeType` + `responseSchema` na Gemini. Idan har yanzu kana ganin wannan a v3.x+, da fatan za ka shigar da rahoton matsala.

## Saitunan Juriya

### Iyakance saurin atomatik ba ya kunnawa

- Iyakance saurin atomatik yana aiki ne kawai ga masu samarwa masu amfani da maɓallin API (ba OAuth/biyan kuɗi ba)
- Tabbatar cewa an kunna iyakance saurin atomatik a **Settings → Resilience → Provider Profiles**
- Duba ko mai samarwar yana dawo da lambobin matsayi na `429` ko kanun `Retry-After`

### Daidaita jinkirin ƙaruwa ninki-ninki

Bayanan martabar masu samarwa suna goyon bayan waɗannan saituna:

- **Jinkirin tushe** — Lokacin jira na farko bayan gazawar farko (tsoho: 1s)
- **Matsakaicin jinkiri** — Iyakar mafi tsawon lokacin jira (tsoho: 30s)
- **Mai ninkawa** — Adadin ƙara jinkiri ga kowace gazawa mai jere (tsoho: 2x)

### Hana cunkoson buƙatu na lokaci guda

Lokacin da buƙatu masu yawa na lokaci guda suka isa ga mai samarwa da aka iyakance saurinsa, OmniRoute yana amfani da mutex + iyakance saurin atomatik don jera buƙatun tare da hana gazawa mai yaɗuwa. Wannan yana faruwa ta atomatik ga masu samarwa masu amfani da maɓallin API.

### Buƙatun taɗi suna gazawa da 503 / chat_admission_busy

**Alamomi:**

- Mahaɗin kammala taɗi yana dawo da amsar `503` da za a iya sake gwadawa, wadda lambar kuskurenta ita ce
  `chat_admission_busy`.
- Amsar tana ɗauke da `Retry-After`. Tun daga #12135, ana samo ƙimar ne daga yawan
  amfani da aka lura da shi — mafi girma tsakanin taga ta `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_QUEUE_MS` da buƙatar ta riga
  ta jira da kuma tsawon lokacin da aka riƙe lasisin nauyi na yanzu — ana zagaye shi zuwa cikakkun
  daƙiƙoƙi kuma ana iyakance shi zuwa 60. A ƙofar da ba ta aiki, yana kiyaye tsoffin ƙananan iyakoki: daƙiƙoƙi 2 a
  hanyar da ta dogara da bytes, daƙiƙa 1 a hanyar da ta dogara da tsari (wadda kuma ta haɗa da
  `reason: "structure_limit"`).
- Wannan na iya faruwa yayin da wani taɗi mai nauyi ko amsar yawo mai ɗaukar lokaci mai tsawo ke ci gaba
  da gudana.

Jikin amsar da ya dogara da bytes shi ne:

```json
{
  "error": {
    "message": "Chat admission capacity is temporarily unavailable. Retry shortly.",
    "type": "server_error",
    "code": "chat_admission_busy"
  }
}
```

Amsar da ta dogara da tsari tana amfani da nau'i da lamba iri ɗaya, tare da saƙon
`Local chat admission capacity is busy for this structurally heavy request; upstream provider routing was not attempted. Retry shortly.`
da `reason: "structure_limit"`.
A tsoffin matakan iyaka, ana ɗaukar buƙata mai nauyi ta fuskar tsari idan tana da aƙalla saƙonni `200`,
aƙalla kayan aiki `64`, ko aƙalla kiyasin token `32,000`, ko kuma idan ƙididdigar tsari mai iyaka
ta kai iyakokinta na kumburai `10,000` da aka ziyarta ko zurfin `12`.

**Dalili:** Wannan zubar da kaya ne da aka yi da gangan a cikin OmniRoute, ba gazawar mai samarwa na waje ba.
Kowane tsari yana amfani da kariya ta cikin-tsari don tanadar iyakantacciyar damar aiki mai nauyi kafin riƙewa
da fassara babban jikin buƙata. Ana ci gaba da riƙe lasisin aiki mai nauyi tsawon rayuwar amsar SSE.

**#503-fanout:** kafin wannan gyaran, kariyar tana iyakance yawan aiki na lokaci guda zuwa tsayayyen ADADIN buƙatu
(`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT`, tsoho `1`) ba tare da la'akari da ƙwaƙwalwar masaukin ba, don haka bazuwar ayyukan
wakilan shirye-shirye (ƙananan wakilai/CLI da yawa, jikunan buƙatu da kullum suke > 256 KB) ta ragu zuwa ingantaccen
yawan aiki na lokaci guda na kusan 1 kuma ta haifar da 503 a ƙarƙashin kaya na yau da kullum. Yanzu kariyar tana daidaita
kanta: kasafin shigar da BYTE (`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_INFLIGHT_BYTES`) da aka samo ta atomatik ne ke sarrafa ta, wanda aka
ƙayyade daga ainihin iyakar ƙwaƙwalwar tsarin, sannan kuma tana duba siginar matsin albarkatu kai-tsaye — don haka
tana zubar da kaya ne kawai lokacin da masaukin ke ƙarƙashin ainihin matsin ƙwaƙwalwa, ba wai don kawai buƙatu masu
nauyi fiye da ɗaya sun iso lokaci guda ba. Har yanzu ana mutunta tsohuwar iyakar adadi
(`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT`), amma sai idan ka saita ta da kanka.

Lokacin da dama take cike, buƙata mai nauyi za ta fara jira har zuwa
`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_QUEUE_MS` (tsoho `2000`, `0` yana kashe jiran) domin wani gurbi ya samu
kafin a dawo da amsar `503` da za a iya sake gwadawa. Wannan jira mai iyaka yana nan ne domin kwastomomi irin na wakili
(OpenCode, Claude Code, Cursor) waɗanda ke baza ƙananan buƙatu masu nauyi a lokaci guda su jera wannan tururuwa
maimakon ƙona dukan kasafin sake gwadawarsu a kan kin amincewa nan take, su kuma tsaya a tsakiyar aiki.
Ana nuna yawan amfani da lasisin aiki mai nauyi na yanzu, kasafin bytes da aka ƙayyade, da tsananin matsin kai-tsaye
a `GET /api/monitoring/health` → `chatAdmission` (`inflightBytes`, `maxInflightBytes`,
`budgetSource`, `pressureSeverity`, `countCapEnabled`) — duba waɗannan kafin canza kowace env var.
Settings → Resilience → Request Queue → Concurrent Requests ba ya sarrafa wannan; wannan saitin
yana kula da wata dabam hanyar jerin-gwanon buƙatun mai samarwa.

**Gyara:**

1. Fara da sake gwadawa. Ya kamata kwastomomi su mutunta `Retry-After` kuma su yi amfani da jinkirin ƙaruwa maimakon sake
   maimaita buƙatar nan take.
2. Duba `/api/monitoring/health` → `chatAdmission` kafin daidaita komai. `countCapEnabled:
false` da babban `maxInflightBytes` suna nufin kasafin da aka samo ta atomatik yana riga yana yin
   aikinsa; `pressureSeverity` na `high`/`critical` yana nufin masaukin na fama da ƙarancin ƙwaƙwalwa da gaske —
   ba za a iya gyara wannan da env var na izinin shiga ba, yana buƙatar ƙarin RAM ko ƙaramin nauyin aiki.
3. Sai kawai idan `/api/monitoring/health` ya nuna cewa kasafin da aka samo ta atomatik ya yi ƙanƙanta da gaske ga
   masaukin ka (abu mai wuya — ya riga yana faɗaɗa daga container zuwa bare-metal), ka maye gurbinsa kai tsaye da
   `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_INFLIGHT_BYTES` maimakon komawa ga tsohuwar iyakar adadin buƙatu.

Duba [manazartar environment-variable](../reference/ENVIRONMENT.md#4-security--authentication)
don ingantattun saitunan izinin shiga.

## Zaɓin RAG / tsarin rarraba gazawar LLM (matsaloli 16)

Wasu masu amfani da OmniRoute suna sanya gateway ɗin a gaban tsarin RAG ko tarin agents. A irin waɗannan saituna, ana yawan ganin wani abin mamaki: OmniRoute yana bayyana lafiyayye (providers suna aiki, routing profiles suna daidai, babu faɗakarwar rate limit), amma duk da haka amsar ƙarshe ba daidai ba ce.

A aikace, waɗannan matsalolin galibi suna fitowa ne daga RAG pipeline na downstream, ba daga gateway ɗin kansa ba.

Idan kana son amfani da kalmomi iri ɗaya wajen bayyana waɗannan gazawar, za ka iya amfani da WFGY ProblemMap, wata madogarar rubutu ta waje mai lasisin MIT wadda ke bayyana nau'ikan gazawar RAG / LLM guda goma sha shida da suke maimaituwa. A taƙaice, ya ƙunshi:

- karkacewar retrieval da lalacewar iyakokin context
- indexes da vector stores marasa komai ko waɗanda suka tsufa
- rashin daidaito tsakanin embedding da ma'ana
- matsalolin haɗa prompt da context window
- rugujewar logic da amsoshi masu nuna tabbaci fiye da kima
- gazawar doguwar chain da daidaitawar agents
- karkacewar memory da roles a tsarin multi agent
- matsalolin tsarin jeren deployment da bootstrap

Manufar mai sauƙi ce:

1. Lokacin da kake binciken amsa mara kyau, tattara:
   - aikin mai amfani da request ɗinsa
   - haɗin route ko provider a cikin OmniRoute
   - duk wani RAG context da aka yi amfani da shi a downstream (retrieved documents, tool calls, da sauransu)
2. Danganta matsalar da lamba ɗaya ko biyu na WFGY ProblemMap (`No.1` … `No.16`).
3. Adana lambar a cikin dashboard, runbook, ko incident tracker naka kusa da logs na OmniRoute.
4. Yi amfani da shafin WFGY da ya dace don yanke shawarar ko kana buƙatar sauya RAG stack, retriever, ko routing strategy naka.

Cikakken rubutu da takamaiman hanyoyin aiwatarwa suna nan (lasisin MIT, rubutu kawai):

[WFGY ProblemMap README](https://github.com/onestardao/WFGY/blob/main/ProblemMap/README.md)

Za ka iya yin watsi da wannan sashe idan ba ka gudanar da RAG ko agent pipelines a bayan OmniRoute.

## Sanannun Matsalolin v3.8.0

Matsalolin da suka shafi sakin v3.8.0 musamman da hanyoyin wucin gadi na magance su a yanzu. Idan an samar da gyara a wani patch na gaba, za a sabunta ko cire bayanin.

### Gazawar auth na Devin CLI

**Alamomi:**

- "Ba a sami Devin CLI ba" ko "auth ya gaza" lokacin kiran tools masu amfani da Devin
- Binciken lokacin gudanar da CLI yana nuna `installed=false`

**Dalilai:**

- `CLI_DEVIN_BIN` yana nuna path da babu shi
- Ba a shigar da Devin CLI a host ɗin ba

**Gyara:**

1. Shigar da Devin CLI na platform ɗinka
2. Saita `CLI_DEVIN_BIN=/usr/local/bin/devin` (ko ainihin path ɗin) a cikin `.env`
3. Sake kunna OmniRoute sannan ka sake gwadawa daga **Dashboard → CLI Tools**

### Model cooldown ya makale (sake saiti da hannu)

**Alamomi:**

- Ana ci gaba da nuna model a cikin cooldown ko da bayan lokacin ƙarewarsa ya wuce
- Requests har yanzu suna tsallake model ɗin a combo routing duk da cewa timestamp ɗin ya riga ya wuce

**Sake saiti da hannu:**

- **Dashboard:** **Settings → Model Cooldowns** → danna **Re-enable** a kan card ɗin da abin ya shafa
- **API:** `DELETE /api/resilience/model-cooldowns` tare da management auth headers

### Haɗin Command Code provider yana gazawa da 403

**Alamomi:**

- 403 yayin gwada haɗin Command Code provider
- Card ɗin provider yana nuna "unauthorized" bayan an ƙara shi sabo

**Dalili:** OAuth flow bai kammala ba (ba a karɓi callback ba ko kuma ba a adana token ba).

**Gyara:**

- Gudanar da `omniroute providers` daga CLI don sake kunna OAuth flow, ko
- Sake gudanar da OAuth daga **Dashboard → Providers → Command Code → Reconnect**

### ModelScope yana mayar da cooldowns na 429 masu tsauri

**Alamomi:**

- Gajerun cooldowns sosai ko na nan take a ModelScope bayan ƙaramin tarin requests
- Combo routing yana tsallake ModelScope da wuri fiye da yadda ake tsammani

**Dalili:** ModelScope yana fitar da headers na `Retry-After` waɗanda suka keɓanta da provider. v3.8.0 yana zuwa da keɓantacciyar hanyar sarrafa waɗannan headers, saboda haka tsofaffin versions suna kuskuren karanta su a matsayin alamomin rate-limit na gama-gari.

**Gyara:**

- Tabbatar kana amfani da v3.8.0 ko mafi sabo
- Tabbatar an kunna toggle na `useUpstream429BreakerHints` a ƙarƙashin **Settings → Resilience**

### Babu OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET a production

**Alamomi:**

- 401 a kan kowane Codex/Responses WebSocket bridge request lokacin gudanarwa a remote production host
- WebSocket bridge handshake yana rufewa nan take bayan haɗuwa

**Dalili:** Babu env var na `OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET` a production environment.

**Gyara:**

1. Ƙirƙiri random secret: `openssl rand -hex 32`
2. Saita `OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET=<random-secret>` a env na production server (da duk wani client da ke sadarwa da bridge ɗin)
3. Sake kunna OmniRoute

### Responses API: an rage background mode zuwa synchronous

**Alamomi:**

- An rubuta warning: `background mode degraded to synchronous`
- Request mai `background: true` yana mayar da amsar synchronous ta yau da kullum maimakon background job handle

**Dalili:** A v3.8.0, da gangan ake rage `background: true` a Responses API zuwa synchronous execution tare da fitar da warning. Cikakken async background execution wani abu ne da za a samar nan gaba.

**Gyara:**

- Daidaita client ɗin don ya yi kira ba tare da `background` ba, ko
- Jira wani release na gaba da zai zo da cikakken async background mode (bibiyi changelog)

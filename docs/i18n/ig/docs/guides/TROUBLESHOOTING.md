## Nchọpụta Nsogbu Zuru Ezu

---

### Ịmachi Ọnụego na Ndị Na-eweta Ọrụ Efuu (429 / 400 / 401)

**Mgbaàmà**: Mgbe ị na-eji `model: "auto"` na ndị na-eweta ọrụ efu/na-achọghị nkwenye (opencode, auggie, wdg.), ị na-enweta `HTTP 429`, `400`, ma ọ bụ `401` mgbe ụfọdụ kama azịza. Arịrịọ ndị ahụ na-aga nke ọma mgbe e zighachiri otu mkpali ahụ obere oge ka e mesịrị, mana akpaaka (ọrụ cron, ndị nnọchi anya, skripti) na-akwụsị na ọdịda mbụ.

**Isi ihe kpatara ya**: Ụdị ọdịda atọ na-anọpụ iche na-agbakọta:

1. **Oke ọnụego onye na-eweta ọrụ (`429`)**: Ọkwa efu nwere ike itinye oke òkè maka windo oge ọ bụla. Ọtụtụ oku e zigara n'otu ntabi anya na-emebi oke ahụ, ya mere a na-ajụ arịrịọ na-esote ruo mgbe windo ahụ malitere ọzọ.
2. **Ụdịdị mebiri emebi na passthrough (`400`/`401`)**: Ọdọ `auto/*` nwere ike ịgụnye ụdịdị passthrough sitere na `opencode` ndị edebanyere n'ime katalọgụ mana enweghị nzere dị ndụ (dịka `oc/north-mini-code-free` → `401`). Auto-router na-anwale otu, ọ daa, a na-agbasa njehie ahụ tupu usoro ịlaghachi azụ amalite.
3. **Mmụba site n'ịrụkọ ọrụ n'otu oge (`429` mgbe ibu dị ukwuu)**: Mgbe ọtụtụ nnọkọ onye nnọchi anya/cron na-akpọ `auto` n'otu oge, mkpokọta ọnụego arịrịọ na-agafe ihe ndị na-eweta ọrụ efu nwere ike ịnagide, ya mere a na-akara oku ziri ezi dịka mmegbu.

**Ndozi a kwadoro (ndị obodo kọọrọ ya, 2026-08-10)**: hazie mgbanwe gburugburu atọ ka ntụgharị, ịrụkọ ọrụ n'otu oge, na ịlaghachi azụ wee nabata mgbanwe na ọkwa efu kama ịkwụsị n'ihi ya:

```bash
export OMNIROUTE_ROTATE_ON_400=true           # gafee na ụdịdị/onye na-eweta ọrụ ọzọ mgbe 400/401 mere (na-amafe ụdịdị passthrough mebiri emebi)
export OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT=4   # oke nnabata doro anya maka ọrụ dị arọ (anaghị edobe ya na ndabara: enweghị oke ọnụọgụ arịrịọ, lee ndetu n'okpuru)
export OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_QUEUE_MS=5000 # ichere nwere oke ogologo karị maka ikike ọrụ dị arọ kama 503 a pụrụ ịnwale ọzọ ozugbo
```

Debe ihe ndị a na gburugburu usoro OmniRoute (daemon ahụ, dịka ọmụmaatụ site na LaunchAgent plist ma ọ bụ `systemctl edit`), wee malite OmniRoute ọzọ. Ọkọlọtọ ntụgharị ahụ bụ ihe kachasị nwee mmetụta: ọ na-agbanwe ọdịda siri ike ka ọ bụrụ nnwale ọzọ na-enweghị onye ọrụ ịhụ ya megide onye na-eweta ọrụ dị mma n'ime ọdọ ahụ.

**Ndetu**: `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT` na-amachi ọnụọgụ arịrịọ dị arọ — ndị nwere ogologo konteks — nwere ike ịrụ ọrụ n'otu oge; oke ahụ bụ ọnụ ụzọ nnabata, ọ bụghị ihe na-amachi ọnụego onye na-eweta ọrụ. **Mmelite #503-fanout:** anaghịzi edobe var a na ndabara (ọ na-amachizi naanị mgbe ahaziri ya kpọmkwem, dịka e gosiri n'elu) — kama nke ahụ, mmefu byte a na-enweta na-akpaghị aka (`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_INFLIGHT_BYTES`) na-achịkwa nnabata ọrụ dị arọ, nke na-agbatị onwe ya dabere na ezigbo oke ebe nchekwa nke host, ya mere nrụnye ọhụrụ kwesịrị ịhụ `503 chat_admission_busy` ole na ole nke ukwuu n'etinyeghị var a ma ọlị; idobe ya kpọmkwem ebe a ka na-arụ ọrụ kpọmkwem dịka edere ya. A na-amachi mgbanwe mmefu byte ahaziri kpọmkwem n'etiti 8 MiB–2 GiB. `413 body_exceeds_budget` abụghị ihe na-adịru nwa oge: bulie mmefu byte ahụ, belata `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_HARD_MAX_BODY_BYTES`, ma ọ bụ bulie oke ebe nchekwa nke usoro ahụ. Mwepụ `inflight_bytes_budget` bụ esemokwu nwa oge ma ka nwere ike ịnwale ọzọ. `RATE_LIMIT_MAX_WAIT_MS`, `RATE_LIMIT_MAX_QUEUE_DEPTH`, na `RATE_LIMIT_AUTO_ENABLE` na-achịkwa ịmachi ọnụego maka onye na-eweta ọrụ ọ bụla (`open-sse/services/rateLimitManager.ts`) iche — lee `.env.example`.

**Otu esi enyocha na ọ rụrụ ọrụ**: mee ka onye nnọchi anya/cron gị gbaa ugboro abụọ n'usoro ngwa ngwa ma gosi na ha abụọ gara nke ọma. Tupu ndozi ahụ, agba nke abụọ na-atụpụkarị `429`/`401`. Mgbe ndozi ahụ gasịrị, a na-anwale ọdịda (ma ọ bụrụ na ọ bụla) ọzọ n'enweghị onye ọrụ ịhụ ya, oku ahụ wee mechaa. Ị nwekwara ike iji `curl /monitoring/health` wee lelee oghere `rateLimitedUntil` na njikọ ndị na-eweta ọrụ yana `circuitBreakers.providerBreakers[].state` maka ndị na-eweta ọrụ nsogbu ahụ metụtara — steeti ahụ bụ otu n'ime `CLOSED`, `DEGRADED`, `OPEN`, ma ọ bụ `HALF_OPEN` (lee `src/shared/utils/circuitBreaker.ts`), onye na-eweta ọrụ nke na-anọgide na-ada ga-agbanwe `CLOSED → DEGRADED → OPEN` tupu windo nrụpụta ekwe ka nyocha gafee (`HALF_OPEN`).

**Ọ bụrụ na ị ka na-ahụ 429**: akaụntụ na-arụ ọrụ maka onye na-eweta ọrụ ahụ ejirila _quota_ ya niile n'ezie (ọ bụghị naanị oke ọnụego arịrịọ). Tinye akaụntụ nke abụọ maka otu onye na-eweta ọrụ ahụ na dashboard OmniRoute → Providers → Accounts, ma ọ bụ tinye onye ọzọ na-eweta ọrụ n'efu (dịka `routeway`, `auggie`). Ịtụgharị akaụntụ na-enyere naanị aka maka nsogbu ọnụego/400/401 na-adịru nwa oge; mgbe quota gwụrụ kpamkpam, ọ chọrọ credential nke abụọ ma ọ bụ onye na-eweta ọrụ ọzọ.

**Ọ bụrụ na ị hụ 403 na ụdị vision (`auto/vision`, `bazaarlink/*`)**: akaụntụ ejikọrọ enweghị atụmatụ akwụ ụgwọ nke gụnyere vision, ma ọ bụ API key ahụ enweghị ikike zuru oke. Nyochaa na dashboard onye na-eweta ọrụ na scope nke key ahụ gụnyere vision/multimodal, ma ọ bụ jikọọ akaụntụ ọkwa akwụ ụgwọ ma debe ya dịka ebumnuche vision.

## Ịdọ Aka Ná Ntị nke npm install (ERESOLVE / peer / deprecated)

**Ọ dịghị ihe ị ga-eme** — enweghị ike imechi ịdọ aka ná ntị ndị a kpamkpam ma ọ bụrụ na e meghị fork nke ngwungwu upstream.

## Nchọpụta Ụgha nke Antivirus

<a name="antivirus-false-positives"></a>

### Avast/AVG na-etinye `README.md` na quarantine dịka `MD:HttpRequest-inf[Susp]`

**Nke a bụ nchọpụta ụgha. Ọ dịghị ihe butere ọrịa, ọ dịghịkwa ihe ọ bụla a chọrọ ka e mee.**

Avast na AVG na-eji usoro heuristic nke na-akara faịlụ plain-text/Markdown nwere ọtụtụ
njikọ yiri arịrịọ HTTP dịka ihe egwu. `README.md` nke OmniRoute na-esonye n'ime ngwugwu npm (e
depụtara ya na `package.json` → `files`), ya mere ọ na-abanye na `node_modules/omniroute/README.md` mgbe
e mere nrụnye global — ọ nwekwara ihe atụ `http://localhost:20128/...` ruru ~15 (ebe njedebe MCP
HTTP/SSE, URL A2A `.well-known`, na iberibe `curl`). Ọnụọgụ njikọ ndị a
ezuru iji kpalite usoro heuristic ahụ.

Ọ bụrụ na nke a malitere naanị n'oge na-adịbeghị anya: ụdị faịlụ ahụ agbanwebeghị. README gbasaara
tebụl ebe njedebe ya (agbakwunyere MCP HTTP + SSE + A2A) tinyekwara ọtụtụ ihe atụ `curl`, nke mere ka
ọ gafee oke ahụ.

Faịlụ ahụ bụ naanị akwụkwọ nkọwa na-adịghị arụ ọrụ, na-enweghị ọdịnaya ọ bụla enwere ike imepụ. Ị nwere ike iweghachi ya
n'enweghị nsogbu site na quarantine.

**Ihe ị ga-eme:**

1. **Kwụsị ọkwa ndị ahụ** — wepụ ndekọ nrụnye ahụ na nyocha antivirus gị
   (Avast: Settings → Exceptions), site n'ịgbakwunye ụzọ global `node_modules` gị na/ma ọ bụ
   ndekọ data OmniRoute (`~/.omniroute/`).
2. **Kpesa nchọpụta ụgha ahụ** — <https://www.avast.com/false-positive-file-form.php>,
   ma tinye `README.md` e debere na quarantine. Nke a bụ ndozi ga-enyere onye ọ bụla aka, ebe ọ bụ
   usoro heuristic nke onye na-emepụta ya na-emeghachi omume gabiga ókè n'ebe faịlụ ederede nọ.

**Ihe mere anyị anaghị “edozi” nke a n'akụkụ anyị:** ihe atụ niile bụ `http://localhost`, ebe
localhost enweghị ike ịbụ `https` na-enweghị nsogbu nke asambodo aka onye nwe ya bịanyere. Ịgbagọ akwụkwọ nkọwa ahụ iji
zere usoro heuristic nke otu onye na-emepụta ga-emebi ahụmịhe onye ọ bụla na-agụ ya naanị iji mee ka ntụpọ dị na scanner nwee afọ ojuju.

### Kaspersky na-akara ngwa Desktop dịka `PDM:Trojan.Win32.Generic`

**Nke a bụ nchọpụta ụgha sitere na usoro heuristic nke omume. Ọ dịghị ihe butere ọrịa.** Prefix `PDM:`
nke Kaspersky pụtara na mkpebi ahụ sitere na Proactive Defense Module (System Watcher) ya,
nke na-ekpe ikpe dabere n'ihe installer ahụ _na-eme_ kama iji ya tụnyere malware ndị amarala. Mgbe
ọ kpalitere, Kaspersky na-eme “rollback” nke nrụnye niile — na-ehichapụ faịlụ ndị ọ
dereworị — ya mere ngwa ahụ na-emecha mebie ma ọ bụ fuo.

Faịlụ ndị ọ na-akara bụ akụkụ ọkọlọtọ nke dependencies open-source ndị ekwuputara, nke ejikọtara na
ngwa desktop ahụ, dịka ọmụmaatụ:

- `resources/app/.build/next/node_modules/playwright-<hash>/lib/…/agentParser.js` na
  `workerProcessEntry.js` — [Playwright](https://playwright.dev), library akpaaka-browser
  eji maka nbanye provider n'ime ngwa na chat nke browser na-akwado.
- `resources/app/.build/next/node_modules/@wreq-js/binding-win32-<arch>-msvc-<hash>/wreq-js.win32-<arch>-msvc.node`
  — native binding `wreq-js` a kpọchiri n'otu ụdị, nke eji maka HTTP nwere fingerprint browser n'ahụ ndị provider
  na-eji web-cookie (`<arch>` bụ `x64` ma ọ bụ `arm64`).

**Ihe mere ọ ji akpalite:** installer Windows ahụ **enwebeghị code-signing**, ya mere installer NSIS
na-enweghị mbinye aka enweghị aha ọma ọ bụla, usoro heuristic nke omume na-arụkwa ọrụ n'ike kachasị elu. Mgbe e jikọtara nke a
na native DLL etinyere n'ime ya na ọtụtụ narị faịlụ `.js` edere n'okpuru
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\OmniRoute` (gụnyere ndekọ ngwugwu nwere suffix hash sitere na
Next.js standalone build), nke ahụ ezuru iji kpalite usoro heuristic ahụ. A na-eme atụmatụ code signing;
ruo mgbe e mezuru ya, releases ọhụrụ nwere ike imeghachi nke a.

**Ihe ị ga-eme:**

1. **Buru ụzọ nyochaa download gị** (nke a ga-ewepụ ohere na e metụrụ faịlụ ahụ aka n'ụzọ ọjọọ). Release ọ bụla na-ebipụta
   `latest.yml`, nke field `sha512` ya (base64) na-ekpuchi installer `OmniRoute.Setup.<version>.exe`.
   Na PowerShell, site na folda nwere installer ahụ:
   ```powershell
   $b = [System.Security.Cryptography.SHA512]::Create().ComputeHash(
     [System.IO.File]::ReadAllBytes("$PWD\OmniRoute.Setup.<version>.exe"))
   [Convert]::ToBase64String($b)
   ```
   Output ahụ ga-adakọrịrị na `latest.yml` → `sha512`. Ọ bụrụ na ọ dakọghị, hichapụ faịlụ ahụ ma
   budata ya ọzọ naanị site na [GitHub releases page](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/releases).
2. **Weghachi + wepụ na nyocha** — weghachi ihe ndị rollback wepụrụ site na quarantine ma gbakwunye exclusion
   maka `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\OmniRoute` (Kaspersky → Settings → Threats and Exclusions),
   wee wụnye ya ọzọ.
3. **Kpesa nchọpụta ụgha ahụ** — <https://opentip.kaspersky.com/>. Akụkọ FP ndị ọrụ zigara
   na-eme ka itinye na allowlist gaa ngwa ngwa n'ezie.

## Ndakọrịta Node.js

<a name="nodejs-compatibility"></a>

### Ihu nbanye na-akụda ma ọ bụ na-egosi njehie "Module self-registration"

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Ị na-eji ụdị Node.js na-abụghị ụdị kacha nta nke gburugburu ọrụ nchekwa OmniRoute kwadoro. Ọnọdụ a na-ahụkarị bụ iji ọkwa patch ochie nke Node 22 ma ọ bụ 24, nke dị n'okpuru ọkwa nchekwa emeziri nke OmniRoute chọrọ.

**Mgbaàmà:**

- Ihu nbanye na-egosi ihuenyo efu ma ọ bụ njehie sava
- Console na-egosi `Error: Module did not self-register` ma ọ bụ njehie yiri ya metụtara njikọ native
- Ihu nbanye na-egosi **ọkwa ịdọ aka ná ntị oroma** nke nwere ụdị Node gị ma ọ bụrụ na gburugburu ọrụ ahụ anọghị n'ime iwu nchekwa akwadoro

**Ndozi:**

1. Wụnye ụdị Node.js LTS akwadoro (nke a tụrụ aro: Node.js 24.x):
   ```bash
   nvm install 24
   nvm use 24
   ```
2. Nyochaa ụdị gị: `node --version` kwesịrị igosi `v24.0.0` ma ọ bụ nke ọhụrụ karịa n'ahịrị 24.x LTS
3. Wụnye OmniRoute ọzọ: `npm install -g omniroute`
4. Malitegharịa: `omniroute`

> **Ụdị nchekwa akwadoro:** `>=22.22.2 <23` ma ọ bụ `>=24.0.0 <27`. A na-akwado Node.js 24.x LTS (Krypton) na Node.js 26 n'ụzọ zuru ezu.

### npm v11+: Awụnyeghị `better-sqlite3` (Cannot find module)

<a name="npm-v11-better-sqlite3-not-installed-cannot-find-module"></a>

**Ihe kpatara ya:** npm v11 (nke na-eso Node.js 24+) na-egbochi skript nwụnye maka dependencies nhọrọ
na ndabara. Ebe ọ bụ na edepụtara `better-sqlite3` na `optionalDependencies`
ma na-achọ compilation native (`node-gyp rebuild`), npm na-amafere ya n'enweghị ọkwa ọ bụla.

**Mgbaàmà:**

- Sava na-akụda mgbe ọ na-amalite ma gosi `Cannot find module 'better-sqlite3'`
- `ls node_modules/better-sqlite3` na-egosi "No such file or directory"
- `npm ls better-sqlite3` na-egosi `(empty)`

**Ndozi:**

1. Kwado skript nwụnye ndị ahụ ma wụnye ọzọ:
   ```bash
   npm approve-scripts better-sqlite3
   npm install
   ```
2. Ma ọ bụ jiri aka wụnye prebuilt ahụ:
   ```bash
   npm pack better-sqlite3@13.0.1
   tar -xzf better-sqlite3-*.tgz -C node_modules
   mv node_modules/package node_modules/better-sqlite3
   rm better-sqlite3-*.tgz
   ```
3. Nyochaa na ọ na-arụ ọrụ: `node -e "require('better-sqlite3')(':memory:').close(); console.log('OK')"`

### macOS: `dlopen` / "slice is not valid mach-o file"

<a name="macos-native-module-rebuild"></a>

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Mgbe emechara `npm install -g omniroute` zuru ụwa ọnụ, binary native nke `better-sqlite3` dị n'ime package ahụ nwere ike ịbụ nke e mere compilation maka architecture ma ọ bụ Node.js ABI dị iche na nke na-arụ ọrụ na kọmputa ahụ. Nke a na-emekarị na macOS (ma Apple Silicon ma Intel) mgbe binary a rụchara tupu oge eruo adabaghị na gburugburu ọrụ gị.

**Mgbaàmà:**

- Sava na-akwụsị ozugbo mgbe ọ na-amalite ma gosi njehie `dlopen`
- Njehie ahụ nwere `slice is not valid mach-o file`
- Ọmụmaatụ zuru ezu:

```
dlopen(/Users/<user>/.nvm/versions/node/v24.14.1/lib/node_modules/omniroute/app/node_modules/better-sqlite3/build/Release/better_sqlite3.node, 0x0001): tried: '...' (slice is not valid mach-o file)
```

**Ndozi — rụgharịa ya maka gburugburu ọrụ mpaghara gị (ọ dịghị mkpa iweda ụdị Node.js):**

```bash
cd $(npm root -g)/omniroute/app
npm rebuild better-sqlite3
omniroute
```

> **Rịba ama:** Nke a na-emegharị compilation nke njikọ native ahụ ka ọ dabaa na ụdị Node.js na architecture CPU mpaghara gị, wee dozie ekwekọghị nke binary ahụ. Oke ụdị gburugburu ọrụ a na-akwado n'ihu ọha bụ **`>=22.22.2 <23` ma ọ bụ `>=24.0.0 <27`** (`SUPPORTED_NODE_RANGE` dị na `src/shared/utils/nodeRuntimeSupport.ts`, nke kwekọrọ na mpaghara `engines` nke `package.json`). A na-akwado Node.js 24.x LTS (Krypton) na Node.js 26 n'ụzọ zuru ezu na `better-sqlite3` v12.x.

## Nsogbu Proxy

<a name="proxy-issues"></a>

### Nnyocha nkwado onye na-eweta ọrụ na-egosi "fetch failed"

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Ebe njedebe maka inyocha igodo API (`POST /api/providers/validate`) na-agafeghị nhazi proxy na mbụ, nke na-ebute ọdịda na gburugburu ebe a chọrọ ka okporo ụzọ gafee proxy.

**Ndozi (v3.5.5+):** Edozila nke a ugbu a. Ụzọ nnyocha nkwado onye na-eweta ọrụ na-agafe na `runWithProxyContext`, ma na-agbaso ntọala proxy nke onye na-eweta ọrụ na nke zuru ụwa ọnụ na-akpaghị aka.

### Nnyocha ahụike token na-ada na "fetch failed"

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Mmelite token OAuth nke na-eme n'azụ anaghị achọpụta nhazi proxy maka njikọ ọ bụla.

**Ndozi (v3.5.5+):** Onye nhazi oge nyocha ahụike token na-achọpụta nhazi proxy maka njikọ ọ bụla ugbu a tupu ọ nwaa ime mmelite. Melite gaa na v3.5.5+.

### Proxy SOCKS5 na-eweghachi "invalid onRequestStart method"

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Na Node.js 22, dispatcher nke undici@8 adabaghị na mmejuputa `fetch()` e wuru n'ime Node.

**Ndozi (v3.5.5+):** OmniRoute na-eji ọrụ `fetch()` nke undici n'onwe ya ugbu a mgbe dispatcher proxy na-arụ ọrụ, iji hụ na omume ya na-adị otu. Melite gaa na v3.5.5+.

### Proxy MITM n'okpuru WSL: anaghị ejide ngwa desktọpụ dị na onye nnabata Windows

**Ihe kpatara ya:** A na-etinye proxy MITM na asambodo CA ya n'ime gburugburu ebe OmniRoute na-arụ ọrụ. N'okpuru WSL, gburugburu ahụ bụ Linux ọbịa, ebe ngwa desktọpụ AI (Kiro, Trae, Copilot, Zed, …) na-arụ ọrụ na onye nnabata Windows. Ngwa ndị dị na onye nnabata anaghị atụkwasị ụlọ nchekwa asambodo nke ọbịa obi, ha anaghịkwa eziga okporo ụzọ site na proxy sistemụ nke ọbịa, ya mere njide okporo ụzọ desktọpụ anaghị arụ ọrụ ebe ahụ.

**Nkwado:** Gbaa OmniRoute ozugbo na otu OS ahụ ngwa desktọpụ ịchọrọ ijide okporo ụzọ ha na-arụ ọrụ na ya (Windows maka ngwa Windows; otu ahụkwa maka macOS/Linux). Ịhapụ OmniRoute n'ime WSL mgbe ị na-elekwasị anya na ngwa ndị dị na onye nnabata chọrọ ka i jiri aka gị tinye ntụkwasị obi na asambodo CA emepụtara na onye nnabata Windows ma tụọ ntọala netwọk/proxy nke ngwa ọ bụla dị na onye nnabata aka na ebe njedebe proxy WSL — nhazi a anaghị enweta nkwado ma dịkwa mfe imebi.

### Njehie Mmekọrịta Cloud

## Ntọala Nkwụsi Ike

### Mmachi ọsọ akpaka anaghị arụ ọrụ

- Mmachi ọsọ akpaka na-emetụta naanị ndị na-eweta ọrụ na-eji igodo API (ọ naghị emetụta OAuth/ndebanye aha)
- Nyochaa na agbanyere mmachi ọsọ akpaka na **Settings → Resilience → Provider Profiles**
- Lelee ma onye na-eweta ọrụ ọ na-eweghachi koodu ọnọdụ `429` ma ọ bụ nkụnye isi `Retry-After`

### Ịhazigharị exponential backoff

Profaịlụ ndị na-eweta ọrụ na-akwado ntọala ndị a:

- **Oge nchere mbụ** — Oge mbụ a ga-echere mgbe ọdịda mbụ gasịrị (ndabara: 1s)
- **Oge nchere kachasị** — Oke kachasị nke oge nchere (ndabara: 30s)
- **Ọnụọgụ mmụba** — Ogo a ga-eji mụbaa oge nchere maka ọdịda ọ bụla na-esochi ibe ya (ndabara: 2x)

### Mgbochi ìgwè arịrịọ na-abata n'otu oge

Mgbe ọtụtụ arịrịọ na-aga n'otu oge rutere onye na-eweta ọrụ nwere mmachi ọsọ, OmniRoute na-eji mutex + mmachi ọsọ akpaka hazie arịrịọ n'usoro ma gbochie ọdịda na-esochi ibe ya. Nke a na-eme na-akpaghị aka maka ndị na-eweta ọrụ na-eji igodo API.

### Arịrịọ nkata na-ada site na 503 / chat_admission_busy

**Ihe mgbaàmà:**

- Ebe njedebe mmecha nkata na-eweghachi nzaghachi `503` nke enwere ike ịnwale ọzọ, nke koodu njehie ya bụ
  `chat_admission_busy`.
- Nzaghachi ahụ gụnyere `Retry-After`. Kemgbe #12135, a na-enweta uru ahụ site n'ogo ojiji a hụrụ
  — nke ka ukwuu n'etiti windo `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_QUEUE_MS` nke arịrịọ ahụ cherelarị
  na oge e jidela mgbazinye ndị dị arọ ugbu a — a na-akpọlite ya ruo na sekọnd zuru ezu
  ma debe oke ya na 60. Mgbe ọnụ ụzọ ahụ enweghị ọrụ, ọ na-edobe oke ala akụkọ ihe mere eme: sekọnd 2 n'ụzọ
  dabere na byte, sekọnd 1 n'ụzọ dabere na nhazi (nke gụnyekwara
  `reason: "structure_limit"`).
- Nke a nwere ike ime mgbe nkata ọzọ dị arọ ma ọ bụ nzaghachi nkwanye data na-ewe ogologo oge ka
  na-aga n'ihu.

Ahụ nzaghachi dabere na byte bụ:

```json
{
  "error": {
    "message": "Chat admission capacity is temporarily unavailable. Retry shortly.",
    "type": "server_error",
    "code": "chat_admission_busy"
  }
}
```

Nzaghachi dabere na nhazi na-eji otu ụdị na koodu ahụ, yana ozi
`Local chat admission capacity is busy for this structurally heavy request; upstream provider routing was not attempted. Retry shortly.`
na `reason: "structure_limit"`.
N'okpuru oke ndabara, arịrịọ na-adị arọ n'ihe gbasara nhazi mgbe o nwere opekata mpe ozi `200`,
opekata mpe ngwaọrụ `64`, ma ọ bụ opekata mpe token e mere atụmatụ ya `32,000`, ma ọ bụ mgbe atụmatụ nhazi nwere oke
gafere oke ya nke node `10,000` e letara ma ọ bụ omimi `12`.

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Nke a bụ mbelata ibu a kpachapụrụ anya ime n'ime OmniRoute, ọ bụghị ọdịda sitere n'aka onye na-eweta ọrụ upstream.
Usoro ọ bụla na-eji ihe nche mpaghara nke usoro ahụ debe ike dị arọ nwere oke tupu o jigide
ma tụgharịa nnukwu ahụ arịrịọ. A na-ejide mgbazinye dị arọ ahụ n'ogologo ndụ nzaghachi SSE.

**#503-fanout:** tupu ndozi a, ihe nche ahụ na-amachi arịrịọ ndị na-aga n'otu oge site n'ỌNỤỌGỤ arịrịọ edobere
(`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT`, ndabara `1`) n'agbanyeghị ebe nchekwa host, ya mere mgbasa ọrụ
coding-agent (ọtụtụ subagent/CLI, ahụ arịrịọ na-adịkarị > 256 KB) dara ruo na arịrịọ na-aga n'otu oge
nke dị ihe dịka 1 ma weghachi 503 n'okpuru ibu ọrụ nkịtị kpamkpam. Ugbu a, ihe nche ahụ na-ahazi onwe ya: a na-achịkwa ya
site na mmefu BYTE ingest a na-enweta na-akpaghị aka (`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_INFLIGHT_BYTES`) nke a haziri dabere na
oke ebe nchekwa n'ezie nke usoro ahụ, ọ na-enyochakwa mgbaàmà nrụgide akụrụngwa dị ndụ — ya mere ọ
na-ebelata ibu naanị mgbe host nọ n'ezie n'okpuru nrụgide ebe nchekwa, ọ bụghị naanị n'ihi na ihe karịrị otu
arịrịọ dị arọ bịara n'otu oge. A ka na-asọpụrụ oke ọnụ ọgụgụ ochie (`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_HEAVY_IN_FLIGHT`),
mana naanị ma ịtọ ya n'ụzọ doro anya.

Mgbe ike ọrụ juputara, arịrịọ dị arọ ga-ebu ụzọ chere ruo
`OMNIROUTE_CHAT_ADMISSION_QUEUE_MS` (ndabara `2000`, `0` na-agbanyụ nchere ahụ) ka oghere tọhapụ
tupu o zaghachi `503` nke enwere ike ịnwale ọzọ. Nchere nwere oke a dị ka ndị ahịa ụdị agent
(OpenCode, Claude Code, Cursor) ndị na-agbasa obere arịrịọ dị arọ n'otu oge wee hazie mbata mberede ahụ n'usoro
kama imefusị oke nnwale ọzọ ha niile na ọjụjụ ozugbo ma kwụsị n'etiti ọrụ.
A na-egosi ojiji mgbazinye dị arọ ugbu a, mmefu byte e kpebiri, na ogo nrụgide dị ndụ
na `GET /api/monitoring/health` → `chatAdmission` (`inflightBytes`, `maxInflightBytes`,
`budgetSource`, `pressureSeverity`, `countCapEnabled`) — lelee ihe ndị a tupu imetụ env var ọ bụla aka.
Settings → Resilience → Request Queue → Concurrent Requests anaghị achịkwa nke a; ntọala ahụ
na-achịkwa usoro kwụ n'ahịrị arịrịọ onye na-eweta ọrụ nke dị iche.

**Ndozi:**

1. Buru ụzọ nwaa ọzọ. Ndị ahịa kwesịrị ịgbaso `Retry-After` ma jiri backoff kama ịmegharị
   arịrịọ ahụ ozugbo.
2. Lelee `/api/monitoring/health` → `chatAdmission` tupu ịhazigharị ihe ọ bụla. `countCapEnabled:
false` na `maxInflightBytes` buru ibu pụtara na mmefu a na-enweta na-akpaghị aka na-arụlarị
   ọrụ ya; `pressureSeverity` nke `high`/`critical` pụtara na host enweghị ebe nchekwa zuru oke n'ezie —
   enweghị ike idozi nke ahụ site na admission env var; ọ chọrọ RAM ọzọ ma ọ bụ ibu ọrụ pere mpe.
3. Naanị ma ọ bụrụ na `/api/monitoring/health` gosiri na mmefu a na-enweta na-akpaghị aka pere mpe n'ezie maka
   host gị (ọ dị ụkọ — ọ na-agbanwe nha site na container ruo bare-metal), jiri
   `OMNIROUTE_CHAT_MAX_INFLIGHT_BYTES` dochie ya ozugbo kama ịlaghachi na oke ọnụ ọgụgụ arịrịọ ochie.

Hụ [ntuziaka environment-variable](../reference/ENVIRONMENT.md#4-security--authentication)
maka ntọala admission ndị bụ isi a pụrụ ịtụkwasị obi.

## Nhazi nsogbu ọdịda RAG / LLM nke a na-ahọrọ (nsogbu 16)

Ụfọdụ ndị ọrụ OmniRoute na-etinye gateway ahụ n'ihu RAG ma ọ bụ usoro agent. N'ime nhazi ndị ahụ, a na-ahụkarị usoro dị ịtụnanya: OmniRoute na-adị ka ọ dị mma (providers na-arụ ọrụ, profaịlụ routing dị mma, enweghị ọkwa ịdọ aka ná ntị rate limit), mana azịza ikpeazụ ka na-ezighi ezi.

N'omume, ihe omume ndị a na-esikarị na pipeline RAG dị n'okpuru pụta, ọ bụghị na gateway n'onwe ya.

Ọ bụrụ na ịchọrọ okwu ndị otu niile ga-eji kọwaa ọdịda ndị ahụ, ị nwere ike iji WFGY ProblemMap, ihe ederede mpụga nwere ikike MIT nke na-akọwapụta ụdị ọdịda RAG / LLM iri na isii na-eme ugboro ugboro. N'ogo dị elu, ọ gụnyere:

- ntupụ retrieval na oke context mebiri emebi
- indexes na vector stores tọgbọ chakoo ma ọ bụ nke ochie
- enweghị ndakọrịta n'etiti embedding na semantics
- nsogbu prompt assembly na context window
- ndakpọ logic na azịza nwere ntụkwasị obi gabigara ókè
- ọdịda nke ogologo chain na nhazi agent
- ntupụ memory na role n'etiti ọtụtụ agent
- nsogbu deployment na usoro bootstrap

Echiche ahụ dị mfe:

1. Mgbe ị na-enyocha azịza ọjọọ, dekọọ:
   - ọrụ na arịrịọ onye ọrụ
   - route ma ọ bụ ngwakọta provider dị na OmniRoute
   - context RAG ọ bụla ejiri mee ihe n'okpuru (akwụkwọ ewepụtara, oku tool, wdg.)
2. Jikọta ihe omume ahụ na otu ma ọ bụ nọmba abụọ nke WFGY ProblemMap (`No.1` … `No.16`).
3. Chekwaa nọmba ahụ na dashboard, runbook, ma ọ bụ incident tracker nke gị n'akụkụ logs OmniRoute.
4. Jiri peeji WFGY kwekọrọ na ya kpebie ma ị kwesịrị ịgbanwe RAG stack, retriever, ma ọ bụ atụmatụ routing gị.

Ederede zuru ezu na usoro ihe atụ doro anya dị ebe a (ikike MIT, naanị ederede):

[README WFGY ProblemMap](https://github.com/onestardao/WFGY/blob/main/ProblemMap/README.md)

Ị nwere ike ileghara ngalaba a anya ma ọ bụrụ na ị naghị agba pipeline RAG ma ọ bụ agent n'azụ OmniRoute.

## Nsogbu Ndị A Maara na v3.8.0

Nsogbu ndị metụtara kpọmkwem mbipụta v3.8.0 na ụzọ ngafe ha dị ugbu a. Ọ bụrụ na ndozi abata na patch ọzọ, a ga-emelite ma ọ bụ wepụ ndenye ahụ.

### Ọdịda auth Devin CLI

**Mgbaàmà:**

- "Ahụghị Devin CLI" ma ọ bụ "auth dara" mgbe a na-akpọ tools Devin na-akwado
- Nnyocha runtime CLI na-akọ `installed=false`

**Ihe kpatara ya:**

- `CLI_DEVIN_BIN` na-arụtụ aka na path na-adịghị adị
- Awụnyebeghị Devin CLI na host ahụ

**Ndozi:**

1. Wụnye Devin CLI maka platform gị
2. Tọọ `CLI_DEVIN_BIN=/usr/local/bin/devin` (ma ọ bụ path ziri ezi) n'ime `.env`
3. Malitegharịa OmniRoute ma nwalee ọzọ site na **Dashboard → CLI Tools**

### Model cooldown rapaara (ntọgharị aka)

**Mgbaàmà:**

- A ka na-edepụta model n'ime cooldown ọbụlagodi mgbe oge ngwụcha gafere
- Arịrịọ ka na-amafere model ahụ na combo routing n'agbanyeghị na timestamp ahụ dị n'oge gara aga

**Ntọgharị aka:**

- **Dashboard:** **Settings → Model Cooldowns** → pịa **Re-enable** na kaadị emetụtara
- **API:** `DELETE /api/resilience/model-cooldowns` tinyere management auth headers

### Njikọ provider Command Code na-ada site na 403

**Mgbaàmà:**

- 403 mgbe a na-anwale njikọ provider Command Code
- Kaadị provider na-egosi "enweghị ikike" mgbe agbakwunyere ya ọhụrụ

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Usoro OAuth ezughị ezu (anabataghị callback ma ọ bụ echekwaghị token).

**Ndozi:**

- Gbaa `omniroute providers` site na CLI iji kpalite usoro OAuth ọzọ, ma ọ bụ
- Gbaa OAuth ọzọ site na **Dashboard → Providers → Command Code → Reconnect**

### ModelScope na-eweghachi cooldowns 429 siri ike

**Mgbaàmà:**

- Cooldowns dị mkpụmkpụ nke ukwuu ma ọ bụ nke na-amalite ozugbo na ModelScope mgbe obere ìgwè arịrịọ gasịrị
- Combo routing na-amafere ModelScope tupu oge a tụrụ anya ya

**Ihe kpatara ya:** ModelScope na-ewepụta headers `Retry-After` pụrụ iche maka provider. v3.8.0 nwere nhazi pụrụ iche maka headers ndị ahụ, ya mere nsụgharị ochie na-agụ ha n'ụzọ na-ezighi ezi dị ka ntụnye rate-limit izugbe.

**Ndozi:**

- Gbaa mbọ hụ na ị nọ na v3.8.0 ma ọ bụ nke ọhụrụ karịa
- Nyochaa na toggle `useUpstream429BreakerHints` gbanyere n'okpuru **Settings → Resilience**

### OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET adịghị na production

**Mgbaàmà:**

- 401 na arịrịọ bridge WebSocket Codex/Responses ọ bụla mgbe a na-agba ya na host production dị anya
- Handshake bridge WebSocket na-emechi ozugbo njikọ gachara

**Ihe kpatara ya:** Env var `OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET` adịghị n'ime environment production.

**Ndozi:**

1. Mepụta secret enweghị usoro: `openssl rand -hex 32`
2. Tọọ `OMNIROUTE_WS_BRIDGE_SECRET=<random-secret>` n'ime env server production (nakwa na client ọ bụla na-ekwurịta okwu na bridge ahụ)
3. Malitegharịa OmniRoute

### Responses API: e wedatara background mode ka ọ bụrụ synchronous

**Mgbaàmà:**

- Edere ịdọ aka ná ntị: `background mode degraded to synchronous`
- Arịrịọ `background: true` na-eweghachi azịza synchronous nkịtị kama handle nke ọrụ background

**Ihe kpatara ya:** v3.8.0 kpachaara anya wedata `background: true` na Responses API ka ọ bụrụ mmezu synchronous ka ọ na-ewepụta ịdọ aka ná ntị. Mmezu background async zuru ezu bụ ihe a ga-eweta n'ọdịnihu.

**Ndozi:**

- Hazie client ka ọ kpọọ na-enweghị `background`, ma ọ bụ
- Chere mbipụta ọzọ nke ga-ebute background mode async zuru ezu (soro changelog)
